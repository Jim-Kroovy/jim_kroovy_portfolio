# Dynamic Jetpack Rigging

> Technical Animation • Unreal Engine • Control Rig

*Video coming soon.*

## Overview

I designed and authored a procedural jetpack Control Rig that uses multiple interpolation techniques across its IK controls to simulate natural body and limb movement during free flight. With no physical contact points to anchor the character, the system generates responsive secondary motion that conveys momentum, direction, and the instability of moving freely through the air.

I authored the underlying pose-driving systems in C++ for performance and stability. No animation assets were required: artists could create and capture poses directly inside the Control Rig editor, allowing new flight poses—as well as poses for vehicles and weapons—to be configured entirely within Unreal Engine.
