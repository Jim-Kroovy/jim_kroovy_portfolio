## Overview

I built this Character Creator almost entirely from the ground up, owning the sculpting, production topology, rigging, morph targets, materials, and the backend logic powering its UI widgets. I also designed its integration with every animation system I authored, ensuring characters remained fully compatible across locomotion, vehicle driving, combat, and jetpack flight.

I engineered the multiplayer-ready systems for saving and loading character customisation data, and established and managed scalable content pipelines that enabled other artists to add new morphs and clothing efficiently and consistently.
