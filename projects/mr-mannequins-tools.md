## Overview

I designed and developed Mr Mannequins Tools, a Blender add-on awarded an Epic MegaGrant for its approach to creating, editing, and transferring character assets between Blender and Unreal Engine. It provides animation-ready mannequin templates alongside tools for character rigging, retargeting, weighting, shape keys, socket attachments, and reliable mesh and animation transfer.

The toolkit is designed to make a technically demanding workflow feel simple and intuitive. Artists can import Unreal characters and animations into Blender, work with clear production-friendly controls, and export them directly back with minimal setup and no need to rebuild their work in-engine. The add-on handles the complexity behind the scenes, while support for custom characters and reusable workflow utilities extends the same straightforward pipeline beyond the supplied Manny and Quinn templates.
